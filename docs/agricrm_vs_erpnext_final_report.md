# 🏗️ AgriCRM vs ERPNext: Post-Sprint 14 Final Report

**Date:** 21-Feb-2026
**Status:** Post-Sprint 14 (Order Pipeline & Fulfillment Tracking Complete)

## 1. Executive Summary

Over the course of 14 Sprints, we have successfully adopted the core architectural paradigms of **Frappe/ERPNext** and embedded them natively into AgriCRM using Django. 

AgriCRM has evolved from a basic CRUD application into a robust, ledger-backed ERP system. We have successfully decoupled Stock from Billing, implemented an immutable Document State Machine, and established a fully functional Order Pipeline.

**Are we "Good to Go"?**
**Yes, for a Phase 1 MVP.** The system is structurally sound. You can safely procure inventory, process sales, fulfill orders, process payments, and calculate accurate financial/inventory valuations without data corruption or destructive overwrites.

However, compared to the full scope of the ERPNext repository, there are still advanced modules missing that you may want to address in future roadmaps.

---

## 2. What We Have Successfully Replicated from ERPNext

### 🔒 1. Core Framework (The Frappe Way)
- **Immutable Document State Machine:** Documents go through `Draft ➔ Submitted ➔ Cancelled` states.
- **Append-Only Ledgers:** We removed destructive mutations (`.delete()`) for submitted records. Reversals are handled by appending negative ledger entries (e.g., `SalesInvoiceCancel`).
- **Amend Lifecycle:** When a Document is cancelled, it can be seamlessly amended into a new version (e.g., `INV-001` ➔ `INV-001-A`).

### 💰 2. Accounts Module (Double-Entry Ledger)
- **GL Entry Engine:** Every financial transaction posts explicitly to the General Ledger via `GLEntry`.
- **Decoupled Ledgers:** We maintain accurate *Accounts Receivable (AR)*, *Accounts Payable (AP)*, *Cash/Bank*, *Tax*, *Revenue*, and *COGS* ledgers.
- **Clearing Accounts:** We use *Stock Received But Not Billed (SRNB)* for the procurement flow to balance unbilled inventory.

### 📦 3. Stock Module (Perpetual Inventory)
- **Stock Ledger (StockMovement):** All physical ins/outs are recorded in an append-only ledger, with strict constraints to prevent negative stock (`InsufficientStockError`).
- **Multi-Warehouse:** Stock is tracked down to the physical `StockBin` within a `Warehouse`.
- **Moving Average Valuation:** Product valuations automatically recalculate on each inward movement, enforcing accurate Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).

### 🛒 4. Selling & Buying Pipelines 
- **Decoupled Pipelines:** Stock moves via Fulfillment documents (*DeliveryNote*, *PurchaseReceipt*); financials move via Billing documents (*SalesInvoice*, *PurchaseInvoice*).
- **Order Tracking:** *Quotations* ➔ *Sales Orders*, tracked precisely at the line-item level (`delivered_qty`, `billed_qty`).
- **Auto-Creation:** Submitting an Invoice automatically creates downstream Fulfillment records for seamless user experience while keeping the backend decoupled.

## 3. What We Are Missing (The Gap Analysis)

While we have built a solid foundation, ERPNext contains thousands of features. Here are the most prominent missing pieces in AgriCRM that you should consider for future Sprints:

### 💼 A. Advanced Financial Accounting (Accounts App)
1. **Manual Journal Entries (JV):** Ability for accountants to pass manual, multi-leg debit/credit adjustments (e.g., for operating expenses, depreciation, or bank charges). Currently, GL entries are strictly auto-generated by the system.
2. **Chart of Accounts Hierarchy:** AgriCRM uses a flat list of GL Accounts. ERPNext uses a hierarchical Tree (Assets ➔ Current Assets ➔ Bank Accounts).
3. **Advanced Payment Reconciliation:** Tying a single bulk payment to multiple invoices using a dedicated reconciliation tool.
4. **Financial Reports:** Although we have raw GL entries, we lack out-of-the-box generated reports like *Balance Sheet*, *Profit and Loss Statement*, and *Trial Balance*.

### 📦 B. Advanced Stock Operations (Stock App)
1. **Material Transfers:** Ability to move stock physically between Warehouses (e.g., *Main Warehouse* to *Retail Store*) without buying/selling.
2. **Stock Reconciliation:** A distinct document to handle physical inventory audits and post adjustment entries (shortages/shrinkage) to the GL.
3. **Landed Cost Vouchers:** Adding external shipping, customs, or freight costs to the Moving Average valuation of imported stock after the fact.
4. **Stock Expiry & Quarantine:** Automated batch expiry tracking and preventing the allocation/sale of expired stock bins.

### 🏭 C. Manufacturing / Production (Manufacturing App)
- **Bill of Materials (BOM) & Work Orders:** AgriCRM currently handles trading (buy/sell). It does not support assembling raw materials into finished goods and processing the resulting stock/GL transformations.

## 4. Conclusion & Next Steps

**Verdict:** The system is completely sound and production-ready for standard trading operations. The architecture is future-proof because the core ledgers (StockMovement and GLEntry) are properly abstracted. 

If you plan to deploy this now, **you are good to go**.

If you wish to continue development, I recommend prioritizing:
1. **Journal Entries (JVs):** Essential for your accounting team to record non-trading expenses.
2. **Material Transfers:** Essential if you operate multiple physical locations.
3. **Standard Financial Reports:** Building the visual P&L and Balance Sheet from the existing `GLEntry` data.
